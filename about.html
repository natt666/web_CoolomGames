Our Story

2021

The Beginning

We met while studying for a degree in animation, videogames, and multimedia at Teknós, Vic, Barcelona. Some of us worked on Midlander; then, some of us worked on an awful videogame named Love in Motion. Somehow, we all ended up working on Dating Killmulator.

2022

Coolom Games is Born

Honest to God, we just started Coolom Games because we needed to pay taxes and the tax man frightens me. You do not fuck with Hacienda.

2024

Breaking Through

We had to push for the game to come out in October. This started spiraling out of control, and now Dating Killmulator has become the most successful Catalan game on itch.io. We also won some awards!

2025

Dating Killmulator wraps up

Tons of people loved the ending, and just as many messaged us saying they didn't understand it. Naturally, we started working on Season 2.
